WHY NEAR IS PART OF TRIPLEES

A report can be checked beyond the system that created it.

After a reviewer approves a report, TRIPLEES calculates a canonical SHA-256 digest. The reviewer’s NEAR wallet signs a call to the TRIPLEES registry contract. The contract records the report version and digest so the same report can be checked later by anyone with the original file.

01 / USER CONTROL

Your wallet signs

NEAR Connect hands the transaction to the wallet. TRIPLEES does not hold a NEAR private key or sign on the user’s behalf.

02 / CONTRACT RULES

Reviewer permission

The registry accepts commitments only from accounts granted reviewer permission by the contract owner.

03 / PUBLIC CHECK

Verify the exact file

The public verifier recalculates the report digest and compares it with the saved commitment and contract state.

WHAT MAKES TRIPLEES DIFFERENT

Not a token marketplace.
An evidence-to-verification workflow.

Many blockchain climate products center their experience on issuing, trading or retiring digital assets. TRIPLEES focuses on the quality and traceability of the records behind a claim. NEAR is used as a verifiable integrity layer after a human review — not as a source of environmental truth.

01Evidence first, token mechanics second — no token sale, carbon token or marketplace is required for the core workflow.
02Digest-only anchoring — private files and full reports are not placed on the public chain.
03Human-approved commitments — agents cannot approve a report or sign a transaction.
04Independent verification — the public check compares the original report bytes with the committed digest.

Know what the record means.

TRIPLEES is designed to make its limits as visible as its capabilities.

Why does TRIPLEES use NEAR?＋

NEAR provides the public contract layer for approved report-digest commitments. A wallet signs the transaction, the registry records a versioned digest, and the public verifier can check the report later.

Does NEAR store the evidence files?＋

No. Original evidence, report contents, private context and reviewer reasons stay off-chain. The contract stores a digest and minimal identifiers needed to verify report integrity.

Does a verified digest mean a climate claim is true?＋

No. It proves only that the supplied report bytes match the digest recorded for that version. Source quality, methodology, environmental outcomes and credit eligibility still require independent assessment.

Is the NEAR wallet the same as my TRIPLEES login?＋

No. The application session controls access to the workspace. The NEAR wallet is connected separately and is used by an authorized reviewer to sign a report commitment.

Is the NEAR registry live right now?＋

The repository includes the NEAR testnet integration and wallet flow, but a contract ID, deployment and reviewer permission must be configured before real commitments can be submitted. The interface should show the configured state rather than imply a live deployment.

Does this replace X1 EcoChain?＋

No. NEAR report commitments and the existing X1 workflows are separate integrations with separate configuration and contract paths. The NEAR product experience does not silently replace or disable X1.
