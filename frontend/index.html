FAQ

Clear answers about the data.

What the workspace checks, what it stores, and where its limits are.

What is TRIPLEES?

TRIPLEES organizes project records, evidence review, available registry data, and monitoring signals in a traceable workspace.

How does TRIPLEES prevent double counting?

It can flag potential conflicts in records that have been imported or connected. Checks do not cover registries or datasets that are unavailable.

Is the data publicly available?

Public views can show non-sensitive project details and evidence references. Private documents and sensitive information should remain protected.

Can I integrate my own data?

Data imports are available; external connectors depend on the integrations configured for a workspace.

Does TRIPLEES certify credits?

No. The workspace supports review and traceability. It does not certify environmental outcomes or guarantee climate claims.
