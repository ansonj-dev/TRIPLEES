ABOUT TRIPLEES

Building trust in climate claims

TRIPLEES connects project and credit records, supporting evidence, registry imports, monitoring observations, and human review—so people can inspect what supports a claim and what remains unresolved.

Blockchain can preserve a tamper-evident history. Evidence review and monitoring add context; they do not, on their own, prove an environmental outcome.

HOW TRIPLEES WORKS

From data to a reviewable record

An end-to-end workflow designed to support integrity, transparency, and trust.

01

Add project records

Enter project details, upload evidence, and import available registry rows.

02

Extract and compare

Extract candidate claims, compare supplied records, and preserve source locations.

03

Investigate

Run specialized agents when configured; external AI and public research require explicit consent and availability.

04

Review findings

Inspect evidence links, limitations, uncertainty, and decisions from authorized reviewers.

05

Report and preserve

Generate versioned reports. Optional report commitments require reviewer approval and a separately configured X1 contract.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Get the answers you need.

Clear answers about what the TRIPLEES prototype does—and what it does not claim.

More about TRIPLEES →
What is TRIPLEES?

TRIPLEES is a prototype integrity and provenance platform for organizing credit history, evidence review, available registry records, and monitoring signals.

How does TRIPLEES prevent double counting?

It can flag potential conflicts in records that have been imported or connected. Checks do not cover registries or datasets that are unavailable.

Is the data publicly available?

Public views can show non-sensitive project details and evidence references. Private documents and sensitive information should remain protected.

Can I integrate my own data?

Integrations depend on configured connectors. This prototype preview does not imply that external data sources are currently connected.

Is this a real carbon credit marketplace?

No. The initial MVP is not a production marketplace and does not certify credits or guarantee climate outcomes.
